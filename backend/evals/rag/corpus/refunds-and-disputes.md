# Refunds and disputes

## Duplicate charges
If the same payment is debited from a customer's wallet more than once, the duplicate debit is reversed in full within 3 business days of the customer reporting it. No fee is charged for duplicate-charge reversals. The customer must provide the transaction reference of both debits.

## Failed transfers
If a transfer fails but the amount was deducted from the wallet, the amount is automatically returned within 24 hours. If the money has not returned after 24 hours, the customer can raise a dispute in the app using the transaction reference. Disputes for failed transfers are resolved within 5 business days.

## Refunds for cancelled purchases
Refunds for purchases cancelled with a merchant depend on the merchant. EasyPay credits the wallet within 7 days of receiving the merchant's refund. EasyPay cannot issue a refund for a merchant purchase before the merchant confirms the cancellation.

## Chargebacks
Card chargebacks must be requested within 45 days of the transaction date. A chargeback request needs the transaction reference, the merchant name and a short description of the problem. Chargeback investigations take up to 30 days.

## Maker-checker approval for refunds
Refunds above PKR 2,000 require approval by a second operations officer (maker-checker). Refunds of PKR 2,000 or less can be processed by a single officer. A customer may receive at most 3 reversals in one calendar day.
