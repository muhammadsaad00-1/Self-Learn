# Accounts and verification

## Upgrading to Level 2
Upgrading a wallet to Level 2 requires a valid CNIC and biometric verification at a partner outlet. The upgrade is applied within 24 hours of a successful biometric check.

## Loan application documents
Loan applications require the applicant's CNIC and bank statements for the last 6 months. Bank statements must be original PDFs downloaded from the bank; scanned or edited statements are rejected. Document verification takes up to 3 business days.

## Dormant accounts
Accounts without any activity for 12 months are marked dormant. A dormant account can be reactivated by verifying the CNIC in the app or at a branch.

## Account closure
Customers can close their wallet from the app once the balance is zero. Closure is final after 30 days.
