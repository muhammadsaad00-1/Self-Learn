# Mehran Microfinance Bank: products and policies

## Sahulat business loan
The Sahulat business loan is for small shopkeepers and home businesses. Amounts range from PKR 50,000 to PKR 500,000, repaid in monthly instalments over 6 to 36 months. The profit rate is 28% per year on the reducing balance. Required documents: a copy of your CNIC, a utility bill for the business or home address, and bank or wallet statements for the last 6 months. One guarantor with a valid CNIC is required for loans above PKR 200,000. A loan decision is given within 3 working days.

## Late instalment charges
If a loan instalment is paid late, a late payment charge of PKR 500 applies for each late instalment, plus PKR 50 per day after the 15th day. Two missed instalments in a row are reported to the credit bureau (eCIB). Customers facing hardship can request a rescheduling of their loan in the app or at a branch before the instalment is due.

## Women entrepreneur accounts
The Behtar Kal account for women entrepreneurs has no minimum balance, free RAAST transfers, and a 1% lower profit rate on Sahulat loans. It can be opened in the app with a CNIC and a selfie for biometric verification.

## Branch hours
Branches are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, and Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. The chat in the app answers 24/7.

## Complaints and escalation
Every complaint gets a case number. Ordinary complaints are resolved within 7 working days and fraud cases within 10 working days. If a customer is not satisfied with the resolution, they can escalate to the bank's complaint officer and then to the Banking Mohtasib Pakistan.
