# Passenger help guide

## Booking a seat
Seats can be booked in the app, on the website, or at any terminal counter until 30 minutes before departure. Children under 3 travel free on a parent's lap; children from 3 to 10 pay 50% of the adult fare. A booking is confirmed only when it is paid; unpaid reservations are released after 20 minutes.

## Cancellations and refunds
Tickets cancelled more than 24 hours before departure are refunded in full. Tickets cancelled between 24 hours and 2 hours before departure are refunded 75%. No refund is given less than 2 hours before departure or after a missed departure. If we cancel a trip, the full fare is refunded within 3 working days, or the passenger can move to the next available departure free of charge.

## Delays
If a departure is delayed by more than 2 hours, passengers may cancel for a full refund. Delays are announced in the app and by SMS.

## Luggage
Each passenger may carry one bag of up to 20 kg in the luggage hold and one small bag in the cabin. Extra luggage costs PKR 50 per kg, space permitting. Flammable or illegal items are not allowed.

## Terminals and boarding
Please arrive at the terminal 30 minutes before departure with your CNIC; boarding closes 10 minutes before departure. Lahore terminal: Thokar Niaz Baig. Karachi terminal: Sohrab Goth. Islamabad terminal: Faizabad. Multan terminal: Khanewal Road.

## Lost items and complaints
Report lost items or complaints in the app chat with your trip ID. Complaints are answered within 3 working days.
