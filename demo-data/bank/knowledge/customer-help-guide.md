# Customer help guide

## Issuing a cheque
To issue a cheque, write the date, the payee's full name, the amount in figures and in words, and sign it exactly as your signature appears in the bank's records. Draw a line after the payee's name and after the amount in words so nothing can be added. For an account-payee cheque, draw two parallel lines across the top-left corner and write "A/C Payee only": it can then only be deposited into the payee's account, not cashed over the counter. A cheque is valid for 6 months from its date. Never sign a blank cheque.

## Requesting a cheque book
A cheque book can be requested in the app under Services > Cheque book, or at any branch. A 25-leaf cheque book is delivered to the registered address within 5 working days. The first cheque book each year is free; after that a fee of PKR 300 applies.

## Stopping a cheque
If a cheque is lost or was issued by mistake, a stop-payment request can be placed in the app under Services > Stop cheque, with the cheque number. Stop-payment takes effect immediately and costs PKR 200 per cheque.

## Bounced cheques
A cheque is returned unpaid if the account has insufficient funds, the signature doesn't match, or the cheque is older than 6 months. A returned-cheque fee of PKR 500 is charged to the issuer. Issuing a cheque without sufficient funds can be a criminal offence in Pakistan.

## Sending money: RAAST and IBFT
RAAST transfers to any bank account or RAAST ID (mobile number) are free and arrive within seconds, 24/7. IBFT (inter-bank funds transfer) to another bank arrives within minutes and costs PKR 0 up to PKR 25,000 per month, then 0.1% of the amount. The daily transfer limit is PKR 500,000 for verified accounts and PKR 50,000 for basic accounts.

## Failed transfers
If a transfer was debited from your account but did not reach the recipient, the beneficiary bank has either rejected it (for example an invalid or closed account) or it is still in transit. A transfer the network reports as failed is reversed to your account immediately once you report it, or automatically within 24 hours. A transfer still in transit after 24 hours is investigated with the beneficiary bank through a formal inquiry; inquiries are resolved within 5 business days. Always double-check the IBAN and account title before sending.

## Duplicate charges
If the same payment is debited from a customer's account more than once, the duplicate debit is reversed in full within 3 business days of the customer reporting it. No fee is charged for duplicate-charge reversals.

## Maker-checker approval for refunds
Refunds above PKR 2,000 require approval by a second operations officer (maker-checker). Refunds of PKR 2,000 or less can be processed by a single officer. A customer may receive at most 3 reversals in one calendar day.

## Fraud and unauthorized transactions
If you see a payment you did not make, report it immediately in the chat. The card is frozen at once to stop further losses, and a fraud specialist investigates the disputed transactions within 10 business days. Never share your OTP, PIN or CVV with anyone, including callers who claim to be from the bank: the bank never asks for them.

## Freezing and unfreezing a card
A card can be frozen and unfrozen instantly in the app under Cards > Freeze. A frozen card blocks new payments and ATM withdrawals; incoming transfers still arrive. After a fraud investigation, the card is unfrozen or replaced free of charge.

## Account statements
Account statements for any period in the last 5 years can be downloaded in the app under Accounts > Statements, as PDF. A stamped statement for a visa or loan application can be requested at a branch for PKR 250.

## Microfinance loans
Small business and personal loans from PKR 10,000 to PKR 500,000 are available to account holders with at least 3 months of account history. Applications are made in the app; a decision is given within 48 hours. Repayment is in monthly instalments over 3 to 24 months.
