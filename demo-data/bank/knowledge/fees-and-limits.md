# Fees and limits

## Transfer fees
IBFT transfers to other banks are free for the first 5 transfers each month; after that each IBFT transfer costs PKR 15. Transfers between EasyPay wallets are always free.

## Cash withdrawal fees
Cash withdrawal at partner ATMs costs PKR 25 per withdrawal. Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs cost PKR 35.

## Transaction limits
Level 1 wallets can send up to PKR 50,000 per day and PKR 200,000 per month. Level 2 wallets can send up to PKR 500,000 per month. Limits reset at midnight Pakistan time.

## Bill payments
Utility bill payments are free. A bill payment that fails is reversed to the wallet within 24 hours.
