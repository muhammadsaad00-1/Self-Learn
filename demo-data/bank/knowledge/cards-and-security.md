# Cards and security

## Lost or stolen cards
Report a lost or stolen card immediately in the app or by calling the helpline at 111-327-729. The card is blocked within 1 hour of the report. A replacement card costs PKR 500 and is delivered within 7 business days.

## Unauthorized transactions
Transactions the customer did not authorize must be reported within 30 days. Fraud cases are investigated within 10 business days. Provisional credit may be given for amounts up to PKR 50,000 while the investigation is open.

## Card blocked after wrong PIN
A card is blocked automatically after 3 wrong PIN attempts. The customer can unblock it in the app after verifying with an OTP, or by visiting a branch with their CNIC.

## OTP and login problems
OTPs are sent by SMS and are valid for 5 minutes. If the OTP does not arrive within 2 minutes, the customer can request a new one; at most 5 OTPs can be requested per hour. Customers who changed their phone number must update it at a branch.
